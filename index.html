Online Automatic synthesis
For multimodal LLM evaluation framework, HY-MAST provides audio, symbolic sheet, and theory articles. Audio music is derived from two sources: automatically synthesized standard music samples and authentic human-composed works. Due to the lack of a well-structured music synthesis system in previous works, HY-MAST incorporates a complete set of tools for on-line synthesis of standard samples. Guided by the rigorous norms of musicological theory, it can automatically synthesize accurate standard music samples (No AI) in accordance with specified instructions. Synthesized files are provided in WAV, MIDI, ABC and XML formats, which can be applied to LLM benchmark construction, music generation samples, and art examinations.

AI Melody Generation and Automatic Audio Rendering
For the questions of Melody Transcription and Time Signature Recognition tasks, the target melody to be recognized is generated by an LM. The LM model first produces content in ABC notation, which is then automatically converted to MIDI and rendered into WAV format by fluidsynth, before being finally supplied to the question system. The pipeline for LM-generated melodies is also controlled by the random seed to ensure reproducibility.

Three-dimensional random chain
To ensure that the benchmark system can be controlled by the user-specified random seed, we have also developed a comprehensive set of random selection and random number generation tools. We adopt the "Three-dimensional random chain" method to generate a local random number matrix for all sub-questions, thereby constraining the content of each sub-question and the generation process of its associated music. The usage of this function is as follows:
seeds_chain_dict, Seed_Chain_Matrix = pseudorandom_tools.generate_3d_random_chain(input_seed, [number_of_questions_type, number_of_each_questions, num_process])
Here, the matrix **Seed_Chain_Matrix** represents the local random number for the k-th category, l-th sub-question, and p-th process (batch).
